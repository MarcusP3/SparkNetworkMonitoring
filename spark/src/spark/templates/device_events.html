Event alerts

Optional, and only ever adds: the events above are kept and listed whether or not one matches here, and the rest of SPARK's alerts (Alerts → Rules) are not touched. Add one for each kind of event you want a message about. The first match opens an incident and sends one message; more while it is open are counted on it; it ends, and says so, once none has come for its quiet minutes. Muted devices and maintenance windows hold these like any other alert.
