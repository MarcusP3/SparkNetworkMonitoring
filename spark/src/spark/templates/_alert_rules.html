What was sent, and whether Discord took it, is under Messages.

What is sent
A target going down (after its failure threshold) and coming back, with how long it was down. An SNMP device silent for three polls (at least three minutes) and answering again. The SNMP alerts below: starred ports, CPU, memory and temperature. New devices found by a sweep, in one message per sweep.
What is not
A target whose failure is explained by one it depends on being down — the switch goes, you get one message, not thirty. Degraded, which is a warning on the page rather than a page for you. SNMP silence on a device a target already reports down. The devices from the very first sweep, which are all new and none of them news. Anything on the mute list.
Quiet hours
Alerts that happen in the window are held, and arrive as one summary when it ends. Times are in the time zone set under Preferences.
If Discord is down
Alerts wait and retry — after 30 seconds, 2, 10 and 30 minutes — and are marked failed after that. A webhook Discord says does not exist is marked failed straight away. A burst of four or more goes as one message.

Storage

Read every 5 minutes: TrueNAS pools and drives, Proxmox storage and drives, and disk space on anything running net-snmp.

DEGRADED, FAULTED, UNAVAIL: straight away, and again when it is ONLINE. Proxmox: also a storage that is not available.
% full or more TrueNAS pools, and Proxmox storage.
% full or more Filesystems on Linux servers and appliances.
°C or more for minutes

APIs

Checked every 5 minutes once its certificate is trusted: two failed checks in a row, and again when it works. See Credentials.
TrueNAS: not ONLINE in its pool, or any read, write or checksum errors. Proxmox: SMART health is not PASSED. Again once it is healthy.
Warning and above, once each (SMART failures among them), and again when TrueNAS clears it. Info is shown on the device page only.
Proxmox: not running on two checks in a row, or no longer listed. Watch guests on the device page.

Internet

No public resolver answers and the web check fails, on two checks a minute apart; again when it is back. Not sent when a target watching the gateway is already down. See the Internet card on the Dashboard.
