Channels

Where alerts go. Every channel gets its own copy and is sent, retried and given up on by itself: when the internet is down, Discord cannot be reached and an ntfy server on your network still can. Each says what it gets. What is decided at all (recoveries, new devices, sign-in events, quiet hours) is set under Rules, and how each message fared is under Messages.
